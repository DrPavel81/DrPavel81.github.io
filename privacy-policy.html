This policy covers the Android, iOS and desktop game. Mobile editions offer optional rewarded ads; the desktop/Steam edition does not initialize the advertising SDK. No game account is required.

YOUR GAME DATA

The game stores scores, Coins, cosmetic unlocks, your selections and settings on your device. Local reward records prevent duplicate ad bonuses. We do not operate a game-account server, cloud save, online leaderboard or separate gameplay analytics service.

We do not receive your local save automatically. Your operating system's backup or restore settings may affect local app data.

MOBILE ADVERTISING

Android and iOS use Google AdMob and its consent tools. These services may process IP addresses (including approximate location), device or advertising identifiers where available, ad interactions and diagnostic data for ads, measurement and fraud prevention.

Consent tools can contact Google when the app starts, even if you do not watch an ad. Refusing personalized advertising does not necessarily prevent all advertising-related data processing.

YOUR AD CHOICES

Rewarded ads are optional. The game does not require you to accept personalized advertising to play, and does not reward consent to tracking.

Where required, Google's consent form presents your choices. AD PRIVACY in the main menu reopens privacy options when the SDK makes them available. Device settings also provide advertising controls.

Google's policy describes its processing, retention, international transfers and safeguards:
https://policies.google.com/privacy

SHARING A RIDE

A short gameplay replay is kept in memory to prepare highlights. Screenshots and videos are created locally when you use Share Ride. They contain gameplay and score information, not microphone or camera recordings.

Files are sent to another app only when you choose a sharing destination. That service's own policy then applies. Saving through the system share sheet may store a copy in your photo library. We do not automatically upload your ride.

RETENTION AND DELETION

Local saves remain until app data is removed; uninstalling may not remove OS backups or exported media. Clearing app data also removes local progress and Coins. Delete shared or exported copies separately in the destination app or photo library.

The game removes its known temporary share files older than 24 hours when you next prepare a share. Ad reward tickets without confirmed rewards expire after seven days; unresolved earned rewards are retained for recovery. Google applies its own retention rules.

CONTACT AND YOUR RIGHTS

If you contact us, we use your message and reply address to handle your request and retain them only as needed for support or applicable legal obligations. Never send passwords or sensitive information in a support message.

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict personal data, object to processing, withdraw consent, request portability and complain to a supervisory authority. Contact the publisher shown on the first page. We cannot retrieve a save held only on your device.

LEGAL BASIS AND UPDATES

Where applicable, processing needed to provide requested features is based on providing the service; necessary support and security processing relies on legitimate interests or legal obligations. Consent is used where required for advertising or device access. Google's policies and consent notices explain its role and processing.

If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact the publisher. Material changes will be reflected in this policy and its updated date. New consent will be requested where required.
